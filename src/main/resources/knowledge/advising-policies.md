# Academic Advising Office: Policies and FAQ

This is the knowledge source for the advising assistant. Each "##" section is one topic.

## Office hours and locations

The advising office is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and closed on weekends and university holidays.
Advisors only see students during the appointment slots they publish, so book a slot instead of walking in.

- Maria Chen, Computer Engineering advisor, ENG 281.

Go to the office location of the advisor you booked with.

## Services

Every service is free of charge.

- Graduation Check (30 minutes): an audit of your remaining major and GE requirements before you apply to graduate.
- Major Declaration (30 minutes): declare or change your major and review the required course roadmap.
- Academic Probation Review (45 minutes): build a recovery plan if your GPA is below 2.0.
- Course Planning (30 minutes): plan next semester's schedule and the prerequisite sequence.

## How to book an appointment

Log in as a student, browse the available slots, and book one. You can filter slots by advisor, service and date.
Each slot can be booked by only one student, and only slots that have not started yet can be booked.
You get a booking confirmation notification right after you book.
You can add a short note for your advisor when you book, for example the topic you want to cover.

## Cancellation policy

You can cancel an appointment you booked at any time from My Appointments, but please cancel at least 24 hours before the start time.
When you cancel, the slot becomes free again so another student can book it, and you get a cancellation notification.
Only the student who booked an appointment can cancel it. Advisors cannot cancel on a student's behalf.
Cancel late or not at all and the slot is wasted, so please cancel as early as you can.

## No-show policy

If you do not attend an appointment and did not cancel it, it is recorded as a no-show.
Repeated no-shows may limit how many appointments you can hold at the same time.
If an emergency stopped you from attending, contact the advising office as soon as you can.

## What to bring

Bring your student ID and any documents for the topic.
For a Graduation Check, bring your degree progress report. For Course Planning, bring a draft of the classes you want.
For an Academic Probation Review, bring your recent grades and any notes from your instructors.

## Reminders

The system sends an automatic reminder notification about 24 hours before each booked appointment.
The reminder names your advisor, the service, the time and the office location.
You do not need to do anything to receive reminders. A cancelled appointment gets no reminder.
